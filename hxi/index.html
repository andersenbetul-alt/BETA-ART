HXI is an Oslo-born electronic artist built around speed, distortion and impact. Making Nordic phonk from the north — cold, direct and engineered to move.

From a single track to an international catalog distributed through NCS, the work has reached sync credits, gaming placements and millions of streams. The sound stays consistent: fast, fractured, built for impact.

Oslo isn't the backdrop — it's the reason the signal sounds the way it does. Winter light. Industrial silence. Distance. That's in every track.
