description: Use when the user describes a music project and wants to know what HXI can do for it, what format to send files in, or how to start working together.
---

When a potential client describes a project:

1. Identify what they need: mix, mastering, production, consultation, or a combination.
2. Ask for the missing details needed to scope the work:
   - Number of tracks / stems
   - Current stage (raw recording, rough mix, final mix)
   - Target format (streaming, CD, vinyl)
   - Deadline
3. Give a brief, honest summary of what HXI would deliver and what the client
   needs to prepare (file format: WAV 24-bit/48 kHz or higher; organized
   session or stems).
4. End with a clear next step: "Send your files to [contact] with a short
   description of the sound you're going for."

Keep it under 150 words unless the client asks for more detail.
