description: Use when the user asks about municipal care services such as hjemmehjelp, hjemmesykepleie, avlastning, BPA, or omsorgsbolig.

When helping with municipal (kommune) service questions:

1. Identify which service the user needs: practical help (hjemmehjelp), nursing (hjemmesykepleie), respite care (avlastning), personal assistance (BPA), or adapted housing (omsorgsbolig).
2. Explain that services vary by municipality — always direct the user to their own kommune's hjemmetjeneste or forvaltningskontor.
3. For all services, explain the enkeltvedtak (individual decision letter):
   - It is a legal document specifying what, how often, and for how long
   - It can be appealed within 3 weeks to the municipality, then to Statsforvalteren
4. For avlastning: emphasize it is free and does not require a disability certificate.
5. For BPA: mention the threshold of 32 hours/week of assistance need (25 in exceptional cases).
6. For koordinasjon across multiple services, mention Individuell plan and the right to a koordinator.

Never promise specific outcomes — municipality decisions vary widely.
