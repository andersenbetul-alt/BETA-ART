Framework agreements

Built for the way public bodies actually buy

Municipalities, ministries and health trusts do not buy one picture at a time. They need a supplier who can be called off against a standing agreement, with predictable prices and documentation that survives an audit.

  • Call-off pricing — a fixed rate card for the agreement period, no per-request negotiation.
  • Named categories — the parts of the 35-category directory your organisation actually uses.
  • Representation requirements — briefs written into the agreement, not hoped for.
  • Norwegian language documentation — licence and provenance sheets in bokmål on request.

Assurance

What "verified" commits us to

01

Three tests before catalogue

Provenance, not-reproducible-by-a-machine, and a named buyer. A frame that fails one is never listed — which is why the catalogue is smaller than a stock library and worth more.

02

Documentation as a deliverable

The accession label, capture record and signed licence ship with the file. Provenance written after the question is asked is not provenance.

03

People, not paid models

Fishers, makers, colleagues — photographed at their own work with a signed release within seven days. Nothing staged, nothing borrowed.

04

Commissions on the same standard

When the archive does not hold what you need, we shoot it — and the commissioned frames enter the same verification chain before delivery.

FAQ

Before you write to the desk

Can our legal team review the licence?

Yes, and most do. One document covers territory, term, media and exclusivity, and we return redlines within two working days.

Do you supply imagery for AI training?

No. Every licence excludes use as training data. That exclusion is a reason clients come to the desk, not a detail to negotiate away.

Can you deliver in Norwegian?

Yes. Licence documents, provenance sheets and category names are available in bokmål, which public buyers usually need for their own files.

What if the archive does not hold what we need?

We scope a commission. Shoot days are quoted individually, and the delivered frames carry the same records as archive plates.

How quickly can you start?

A quote within 48 hours, and most agreements start within a week. Commissions depend on season and access — a fishing boat in March is not a studio booking.
