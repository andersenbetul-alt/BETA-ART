§ 05

If you do one thing

Write a deep, honest About page.

Not for SEO, not because it's nice. Because the entire verification thesis collapses without a human face behind it — "Verified Human Photography" from an anonymous person is meaningless. A buyer spending kr 890 wants to know who took it, why, why the archive exists, and why to trust the claim. Every other item on this plan is plumbing. Your story is the product. Give me 10–15 honest sentences about yourself and I'll draft it in the v3 voice, bilingual.

§ 06

What NOT to build (yet, or ever)

The temptation of any plan is to build everything. Resist. A world-class designer's discipline is subtraction.

✕Subscription pricing — destroys the premium thesis. Never.
✕Multi-photographer collective — breaks "verified by one person." Never.
✕AI visual search — overkill under 100 plates.
✕Accounts / login — wait until repeat buyers ask.
✕Print shop — wait until 3 digital sales prove demand.
✕CMS — wait until 20+ plates.
✕Mobile app — browser is enough. Probably never.
✕The second design direction (dark-gold v1) — commit to v3, drop the rest.
