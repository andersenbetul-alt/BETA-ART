QR Codes

Generate print-ready QR codes for the archive, for a specific plate, or for any URL. Scan → land directly on the plate detail page, license options open, buy in one tap. Use them on exhibition labels, business cards, portfolio prints, packaging or catalogue pages.

The archive URL

Auto-detected from the current domain. To generate for a different domain, use "Any URL" instead.

Pick a plate

The QR encodes a deep link to that plate. When scanned, the archive opens with the plate detail panel already displayed and licence options ready.

Any URL

Paste any web address. Use for campaign links, Instagram posts, or a specific hash-linked plate you have opened.

Full URL including https:// is recommended. Very long URLs make QR codes denser and harder to scan.

Notice — You are running this tool from a different domain than betaart.no. QR codes for "Whole site" and "Specific plate" will encode the current domain. Deploy this file on your production domain before printing, or use "Any URL" to point to the correct address manually.
