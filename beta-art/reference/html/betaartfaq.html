§ 01About the archive

Q · 01What is BETA ART?
A curated photography archive where every image is guaranteed to be made by a human photographer with a physical camera. Licences start at kr 190 for personal use. The archive exists to give designers, agencies and publications a source of images that are demonstrably not generated by artificial intelligence.
Q · 02Who took the photos?
[PHOTOGRAPHER NAME], based in Norway. Every plate in the archive was made by the same photographer. This is not a collective, not a marketplace, not a stock library reselling other people's work. One photographer, one vision, one archive.
Q · 03What does "Verified Human Photography" mean?

Three things:

  • Every photograph was made with a physical camera at a specific place and date, recorded in the accession label
  • The RAW original file is archived and available for inspection on request
  • No image in the archive was generated, composited, or altered by artificial intelligence — and by contract, no image you licence may be used to train one

It is a promise you can audit, not a marketing claim.

§ 02Verification

Q · 04How do I know these aren't AI-generated?

Four independent signals:

  • Accession label — camera body, lens, exposure, ISO, focal length, GPS coordinates, and date, published for every plate
  • RAW original — the unprocessed sensor file is archived and available for you to inspect before or after purchase
  • C2PA Content Credentials embedded in the delivered file, cryptographically signed at capture and edit
  • Contractual guarantee — the licence includes a warranty that the photograph was made with a physical camera in a real place; a breach entitles you to a full refund plus damages

Any single signal can be faked. Faking all four is significantly harder than simply not using AI.

Q · 05Can I see the RAW original?
Yes. Write to hello@betaart.no with the accession number and you will get a link to inspect the RAW file. This is offered as a legitimacy check — buyers of substantial licences, agency legal teams, and publications sometimes want to verify before purchase. There is no fee.
Q · 06What are C2PA Content Credentials?
An open industry standard for cryptographically signing image provenance — when it was captured, on what device, and what edits were applied. Every BETA ART plate carries embedded C2PA credentials. Adobe, Leica, Nikon, Sony and the Content Authenticity Initiative all support the standard. Any modern viewer can inspect them.

§ 03Licensing

Q · 07Which licence do I need?

Three tiers, all perpetual and worldwide:

  • Personal (kr 190) — for one individual, non-commercial use. Personal social media, home printing, a private blog
  • Commercial (kr 890) — for one company or organisation. Marketing materials, editorial, print runs up to 5,000 copies
  • Extended (kr 2,900) — unlimited print, merchandise resale, one client sublicence

Full details in the licence terms. When in doubt, ask.

Q · 08Can I upgrade a licence later?
Yes. Pay the difference between what you originally paid and the new tier, and a new licence document is issued for the same plate. Contact hello@betaart.no.
Q · 09How long does a licence last?
In perpetuity from the date of delivery. Once you have licensed a photograph, the right to use it does not expire. What is bounded is how you may use it, defined by the tier.
Q · 10Can multiple people in my company use the same licence?
A Commercial or Extended licence is granted to one legal entity — the buying company. Anyone acting on behalf of that entity can use the photograph within the tier's scope. If two separate companies want to use the same image, each needs its own licence.
Q · 11Can I resell the image?
The photograph itself, no. What you may do differs by tier: the Extended licence permits merchandise for sale (books, prints, apparel with the image) and one sublicence to a named end-client. Selling the image file as a stock asset, or repackaging it in a template product, is prohibited under every tier.

§ 04Ordering & payment

Q · 12How do I order?
Open the plate you want, pick a licence tier, add to cart, and go to checkout. You'll receive a confirmation email within minutes. During the first phase the actual invoice and download link are prepared manually within 24 hours — this is deliberate; every early buyer gets personal attention.
Q · 13What payment methods are accepted?

Depending on where you are:

  • Norway — Vipps, bank transfer (norsk kontonummer), invoice with Norwegian org.nr
  • EU / EEA — bank transfer, SEPA, credit card
  • Rest of world — credit card, PayPal, wire transfer

Business customers automatically receive a compliant invoice with our org.nr and, where applicable, MVA specified.

Q · 14Can I get a refund?

Two situations to distinguish:

  • Right of withdrawal (angrerett) — For consumers in the EU/EEA, Norwegian law grants a 14-day withdrawal right on digital purchases. However, at checkout you consent to immediate delivery, which waives the withdrawal right once the download is sent. If you did not tick that consent, the download waits and you keep the 14-day window.
  • Warranty breach — If we ever fail to deliver what we promised (a human-made photograph with archived RAW), the licence fee is refunded in full plus damages under the licence terms.

§ 05Delivery & use

Q · 15How fast is delivery?
Within 24 hours on business days (Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 CET). The delivery email includes the download link (valid for 30 days), your written licence naming the licensed use, the invoice, and a numbered certificate of authenticity.
Q · 16What file format and size do I get?
Full-resolution JPEG at maximum quality is default — usually 40–60 megapixels depending on the camera used. TIFF is available on request at no extra charge for licensees who need it for print production. RAW files are not delivered under a standard licence but can be inspected for verification.
Q · 17Can I edit the photo?
Ordinary edits are fine — crop, colour correction, brightness, contrast, resizing. What is not permitted, under any tier, is modification that materially alters the character of the photograph (åndsverkloven § 5 — moral rights). Compositing the photograph together with other images so it is no longer recognisable as the original, or altering it to depict something the original did not, needs written approval.

§ 06AI, models & copyright

Q · 18Can I use the photo to train an AI model?
Answer

No. Under any licence tier. Under any circumstance. BETA ART photographs may not be used to train, fine-tune, benchmark, or evaluate any artificial intelligence, machine learning, or generative model. The archive exists precisely because human-made photographs remain distinguishable from generated ones — feeding these plates into an AI pipeline destroys that value proposition, is contractually forbidden, and triggers damages of at least three times the licence fee under åndsverkloven § 81.

If your use case involves any form of automated image analysis where the outputs will be seen by humans, ask before ordering. Some analytical uses (colour extraction for a design tool, subject detection for a search engine indexing your own site) are compatible with the licence. Training a generative model is not.

Q · 19Is there a model release?
Photographs containing identifiable individuals are only offered under Commercial or Extended licences when a signed model release is on file. This is indicated on the accession label as Release · Model release on file. Even with a release, portrait photographs may not be used in defamatory or misleading contexts. Where no release is on file, the plate is only available for editorial use.
Q · 20What if someone uses my photo without a licence?

Two answers depending on which side you are on:

  • If you have licensed a photograph and someone copies your use — that is a copyright matter between you and them, not a BETA ART matter. You have a licence to use the image, not exclusivity in your use.
  • If someone uses a BETA ART photograph without any licence — that is copyright infringement under åndsverkloven. Damages start at three times the applicable licence fee (§ 81), plus injunctive relief and legal costs. We use reverse image search and third-party monitoring to detect this. If you spot unauthorised use, tell us: hello@betaart.no.
