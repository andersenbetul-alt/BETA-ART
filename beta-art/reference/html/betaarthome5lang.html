How each work is verified.

A photograph is only as trustworthy as the record behind it. Every plate carries three forms of evidence — the standard applied to museum holdings.

Method I

The RAW original is archived.

Every plate begins as a RAW file from a real camera. The originals are held in an offline archive and can be inspected on request.

Method II

The capture record travels with the image.

Camera, lens, exposure, place, and date accompany every photograph. Where the equipment allows, C2PA Content Credentials are embedded at capture.

Method III

The licence is signed by the maker.

You license directly from the photographer. Every order arrives with a written licence, a proper invoice, and a numbered certificate — no intermediaries.
