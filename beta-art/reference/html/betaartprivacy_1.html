§ 02What we collect

We collect only what is needed to license a photograph and issue a proper invoice.

When you place an order

By sending the pre-filled order email, you provide:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The company or organisation you are ordering on behalf of (if any)
  • Your invoicing details (organisation number, billing address)
  • The plate(s) you have selected and the licenses you have chosen
  • Your consent to immediate digital delivery and waiver of the 14-day right of withdrawal (angrerett)

When you browse the site

  • Aggregated, anonymised page views via Plausible Analytics — a privacy-first service that does not use cookies, does not track individuals across sites, and does not build advertising profiles.
  • Basic access logs held by our host, Netlify, for security purposes (typically retained 30 days).

In your browser only — never sent to us

Three items are stored in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings:

  • Your shopping cart contents (betaart:cart)
  • Your delivery consent state (betaart:consent)
  • Your language preference (betaart:lang)

§ 03What we do not collect

  • Cookies for advertising or tracking. We use none.
  • Payment card details. Payments, when processed digitally, go through Lemon Squeezy or Vipps. We never see or store your card details.
  • Data from third-party trackers, social media pixels, or advertising networks.

§ 04Why we hold it (legal basis)

  • To provide the licensed photograph and issue an invoice — performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b))
  • To keep required accounting records — legal obligation under the Norwegian Bookkeeping Act (bokføringsloven) (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c))
  • To understand aggregate site use — legitimate interest in operating and improving the site (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f))

§ 06How long we keep it

  • Accounting records (invoices, order correspondence): five years after the end of the accounting year, as required by the Norwegian Bookkeeping Act.
  • Customer contact data: kept while necessary to provide ongoing licensing service. Deleted on request after any legal retention obligation has ended.
  • Analytics data: retained in aggregate only; individual page views are not stored.
  • Access logs: as retained by the host, typically 30 days.

§ 07Where the data goes

Most of our processors operate within the EU/EEA. Where a processor is based outside the EEA (currently: Netlify, Lemon Squeezy), transfers are made under Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

§ 08Your rights

You have the following rights under the GDPR:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — ask us to correct data that is wrong
  • Erasure — ask us to delete data (subject to accounting retention)
  • Restriction — ask us to pause certain processing
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on your consent

Write to hello@betaart.no. We respond within 30 days.

If you believe we have handled your data unlawfully, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet), Postboks 458 Sentrum, 0105 Oslo — datatilsynet.no.

§ 09Security

Your data is kept with reasonable technical and organisational safeguards: encrypted transport (HTTPS), access limited to the photographer, offline backup of core records. If a data breach affects your rights, we will notify you and Datatilsynet within 72 hours as required by GDPR Art. 33–34.

§ 10Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The effective date at the top always reflects the current version. Material changes will be announced on this page.
