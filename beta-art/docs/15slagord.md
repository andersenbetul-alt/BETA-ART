# Short sentences for Beta Art

The lines that introduce Beta Art, in the three languages the project actually
writes in. English is the source. Norwegian is written, not translated — the
same rule the rest of the site follows. Turkish is here because the owner reads
it and will be the one saying these out loud.

Nothing here is new invention. Every line is drawn from copy already on the
site — the guarantee, the approval mark, the archive's description — so the
words a visitor reads and the words in a pitch are the same words.

**One rule for this file:** a line may only claim what the legal notice
undertakes. `claims.py` enforces that for pages; it applies here too, because a
sentence used in a pitch is a promise whether or not it sits in HTML.

---

## 1. The main line

This is the one already on the front door, in every language, and in the
`og:title` of every shared link. It is the slagord.

| | |
|---|---|
| **EN** | Made by a human. Verified at the source. |
| **NO** | Laget av et menneske. Verifisert ved kilden. |
| **TR** | Bir insan yaptı. Kaynağında doğrulandı. |

Why it holds: two short sentences, no jargon, and it survives translation
without losing the claim. The second sentence is the one that does the work —
plenty of people say "made by a human", almost nobody says where you can check.

## 2. One sentence, when someone asks what Beta Art is

**EN**
- An archive of photographs made by people with cameras, where every image can be traced back to its original file.
- A photography archive that can prove nothing in it was generated by a machine.
- Three things under one name: an archive, a studio, and a journal about building both.

**NO**
- Et arkiv med fotografier som mennesker har tatt, der hvert bilde kan spores tilbake til råfilen.
- Et fotoarkiv som kan vise at ingenting i det er laget av en maskin.
- Tre ting under ett navn: et arkiv, et studio, og en journal om å bygge begge.

**TR**
- İnsanların kamerayla çektiği fotoğraflardan oluşan, her görüntünün ham dosyasına kadar izlenebildiği bir arşiv.
- Hiçbir görüntüsünün makine üretimi olmadığını kanıtlayabilen bir fotoğraf arşivi.
- Tek isim altında üç şey: bir arşiv, bir stüdyo, ve ikisini kurmayı anlatan bir günlük.

## 3. The guarantee, said briefly

These carry a commitment, and the legal notice backs each one.

**EN**
- Every plate begins as a RAW file from a real camera, archived within a day with a checksum.
- You license directly from the photographer — a written licence, a proper invoice, a numbered certificate.
- If a plate sold as verified turns out to have been generated, the licence fee is refunded in full.
- Nothing is published here until a person has seen it and approved it.

**NO**
- Hver plate starter som en råfil fra et ekte kamera, arkivert innen et døgn med en sjekksum.
- Du lisensierer direkte fra fotografen — en skriftlig lisens, en ordentlig faktura, et nummerert sertifikat.
- Blir en plate solgt som verifisert vist å være maskinlaget, betaler vi hele lisensen tilbake.
- Ingenting blir publisert her før et menneske har sett det og godkjent det.

**TR**
- Her plaka gerçek bir kameradan çıkan bir RAW dosyası olarak başlar, bir gün içinde sağlama toplamıyla arşivlenir.
- Lisansı doğrudan fotoğrafçıdan alırsınız — yazılı lisans, düzgün fatura, numaralı sertifika.
- Doğrulanmış diye satılan bir plakanın makine ürünü olduğu ortaya çıkarsa, lisans ücreti tamamen iade edilir.
- Burada hiçbir şey, bir insan görüp onaylamadan yayımlanmaz.

## What none of these say, and why

- **No size claim.** Not "hundreds of images", not "Norway's largest". Twelve
  plates are catalogued and none has a photograph yet. The archive's real size
  stays blank until it is real, the same rule the rest of the site follows.
- **No award, no client list, no years in business.** The company is not
  registered yet.
- **Nothing about being first or only.** It is not checkable, and a claim that
  cannot be checked is the opposite of what this brand sells.

When any of those become true, they belong here — with the record that makes
them true, not before.
