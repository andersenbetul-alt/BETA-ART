Find a doctor / Consultation room

Your consultation room

The connection is end-to-end encrypted. Your doctor will admit you when they are ready.

How this call is protected

  • End-to-end encrypted — nobody in between can listen, including us
  • Nothing is recorded unless you and the doctor both agree first
  • You wait in a lobby until the doctor admits you, so nobody else can join
  • No third-party tracking or analytics runs in the room

Who will be present

Before you join

  • Find somewhere private where you can speak freely
  • Have any medicines you take, and recent test results, to hand
  • Tell the doctor straight away if you cannot hear or understand them

If this becomes an emergency

Leave the call and phone your local emergency number. Your doctor cannot send help to you.
