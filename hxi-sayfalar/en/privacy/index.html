PRIVACY

Minimal by design.

This website does not run advertising pixels, newsletter forms or behavioural analytics, and it does not intentionally set first-party tracking cookies.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted through web infrastructure that may process limited technical information such as IP address, browser or request logs for delivery, security and abuse prevention. Those logs are handled by the hosting provider under its own terms and retention practices.

Spotify and external services

The Spotify player is not loaded until you choose to load it. When loaded, Spotify may process data or set cookies under Spotify’s own policies. Links to Spotify, Instagram, YouTube and NCS take you to those third-party services.

Contact

For privacy questions about this site, contact HXI through the official Instagram profile linked from the main website.
