03 / ABOUT

HXI, from Norway.

HXI is a Norwegian DJ and producer active since 2021. The catalog moves across phonk, electronic production and glitch-heavy sound design, with releases including “help urself”, X-PIRATA, AURORA LOVE and MONTAGEM HYSTERIA.
