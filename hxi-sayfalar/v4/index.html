◈ 006 — About

HXI is a Norwegian producer, DJ, and sound designer whose glitch-laced, bitcrushed approach to phonk has made him one of the genre's defining underground voices. Active since 2021, his sound moves between hard phonk, drift trap, and electronic experimentation.

His breakout single 'help urself' surpassed 43 million streams on Spotify — earned organically through the anime and gaming edit community. Zero paid promotion. His music has placed in the Fast & Furious franchise and on the official BODYCAM game soundtrack, where he also handled mix and mastering independently.

A NoCopyrightSounds artist since 2024. Based in Oslo. Active globally.

Independent Artist · Market Position

Major labels control 70% of streaming revenue. HXI operates with full master ownership, 100% royalty access, and direct sync licensing control — in a $37B+ streaming market growing at 15% annually.
