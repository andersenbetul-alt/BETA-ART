Help someone in your own language

Somewhere right now, a person who speaks your language is sitting in a hospital queue, or has given up on being seen at all. Register your specialty, your languages and your licence, and we will route them to you.

Your hours, your fee

You set when you are available and what you charge. There is no minimum commitment and no exclusivity.

Patients arrive prepared

Every patient reaches you having already described their symptoms, how long they have lasted and how severe they are. You start the consultation with the history in front of you.

A flat platform fee

We charge a fixed amount per completed consultation for booking, interpretation and support. We do not take a percentage of your professional fee.

      What happens next

      • We verify your licence with the issuing authority
      • We check your indemnity cover includes telemedicine
      • A clinician on our team reviews your profile
      • You go live and choose your first available hours

      Verification usually takes three to five working days.

      Where you can practise

      Medical licensing follows the patient, not the doctor. You can consult with patients anywhere in the world, but you may only diagnose and prescribe where you hold a licence. We label every session so both you and the patient know which it is before it starts.

      Read how we handle this

      Working with health facilities

      Some countries — Türkiye among them — require remote consultations to be delivered through a licensed health facility rather than by an individual doctor. If that applies where you are, we will tell you during verification and connect you to a partner facility.
