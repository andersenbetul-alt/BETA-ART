Series I is about work — not the polished LinkedIn version, not the stock-library abstraction of hands on a keyboard, but the specific, weathered, sometimes tired reality of people who make something with their day.

Every plate in this series was made on location, with the person's knowledge and signed consent. Their name is on the release; the release is on file. No composite, no stock, no simulation. When you licence one of these photographs, you licence a moment that actually happened — captured with a camera in a place you could go to yourself.

The series is deliberately narrow. Six plates at opening. More will be added as the archive grows. Each new addition is announced to the newsletter first.
