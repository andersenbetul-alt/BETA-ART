# BETA ART Galleri og Utstilling Event — content map

## Owns
Artists, works, series, gallery presentation, exhibition programme, openings, cultural events, venue information and visit/event enquiries.

## Source references
The latest museum/archive page is used only as a visual and accession-language reference. It contains archive/licensing content but no confirmed exhibition calendar, artists, dates, venues or ticket data. The project therefore has a design foundation, not publishable event facts.

## Shared, adapted
BETA ART logo family, seal/aperture mark and source-level verification language. Verification describes the work record; it does not claim artistic quality or legal evidence status.

## Excludes
Private plate licensing checkout, construction documentation, corporate rights desk and B2B industry packages.

## Open production inputs
Confirmed artist names, work records, exhibition dates, venues, ticket/registration links, event images and participation terms.

## Series relation
The series index is retained as a reference for grouping works. It does not provide confirmed gallery artists, exhibitions, venues or events, so no event listing was generated from it.
