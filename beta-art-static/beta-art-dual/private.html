The standard

Evidence before badge.

“Verified Human Photograph” is shown only when the original, maker identity, capture context and rights record pass the required gate. C2PA can strengthen provenance when available; it never substitutes for the rest.

01 · Original

Real original image file supplied and preserved.

02 · Identity

Photographer independently reviewed; no self-verification.

03 · Capture

Date, location/context and available metadata reviewed.

04 · Rights

Licensing authority and relevant releases recorded.

05 · AI disclosure

Fully synthetic imagery excluded; generative changes disclosed.

06 · Conflict gate

Open rights concerns block publication or commercial licensing.

07 · Provenance

Hash/audit record connects the approved file to its archive record.

08 · C2PA

Stored when present as an additional provenance signal.

Licensing

Defined use. Clear rights.

Private, commercial and extended use can be licensed only when the underlying rights record supports it. Copyright remains with the photographer unless explicitly transferred by a separate agreement.

Personal

Private use

Non-commercial personal use. Final pricing and terms remain subject to launch review.

Commercial

Business use

Defined usage, territory, duration and entity scope. No blanket AI-training permission is implied.

Extended / custom

Broader rights

Merchandise, exclusivity or unusual uses require explicit review and written scope.

Future · limited editions

A physical work with a public record.

Signed, numbered physical photographs can use a unique BETA-ID and QR-linked registry. Ownership of the physical print does not transfer copyright. Demo verification is available to test the interaction.
