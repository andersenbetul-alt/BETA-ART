# Verification & Rights Framework — Business reference

Source: supplied pilot draft. This is an implementation checklist, not final legal advice.

Business use: applies to construction/project documentation and Evidence Photos. The badge is not a court or insurance guarantee. Identifiable people require applicable legal-basis and right-to-image review; release evidence must remain private and access-controlled. Unresolved rights conflicts block commercial use. C2PA is supplementary when available.

Required production agreements include contributor, assignment/production, model/property release, privacy, DPA where required and AI/manipulation policy. Qualified counsel must review before paid launch.
