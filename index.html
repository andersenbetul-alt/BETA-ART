About

From Norway. 43 million streams.

HXI is the recording name of Christoffer Andersen, a producer, sound designer and mix engineer from Norway. His 2022 single "help urself" spread through the anime and gaming edit communities on TikTok and YouTube, driven entirely by the audience that found it.

The track has since passed 43 million Spotify streams, establishing HXI as one of the most-streamed phonk producers in Northern Europe.

Built for impact.

Hard bass. Glitch design. High BPM. Every track is produced, mixed and mastered by HXI — no outside engineers. The sound locks to visual content: anime edits, gaming clips, drift videos. That is why it spreads.

It started with one track.

"help urself" spread through fan edits on TikTok and YouTube Shorts. No PR. No budget. 43 million streams on that one track. "X-PIRATA" followed with 2.6 million.

Norway is here.

HXI is part of a growing Norwegian phonk scene. His collab with Slowboy and Crazy Mano on "De Norway Com Amor" connected Nordic and Brazilian phonk worldwide.

No label middleman. Just the work.

HXI is signed to Scar Scheme Records. He owns his masters and handles distribution, licensing and every technical aspect of his releases in-house. Major labels capture roughly seventy percent of streaming revenue on average — HXI keeps his.
