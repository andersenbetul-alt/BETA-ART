    About

    From Norway. 43 million streams.

    HXI is the recording name of Christoffer Andersen, a producer, sound designer and mix engineer from Norway. His 2022 single “help urself” spread through the anime and gaming edit communities on TikTok and YouTube, carried by the audience that found it.

    The track has since passed 43 million Spotify streams — more than every other HXI release combined.

    Built for impact.

    Drift phonk with the Memphis nostalgia taken out. What is left is architectural: sub-bass you feel before you hear it, glitch-heavy sound design, and rhythm cut to lock with a moving image. NCS files “Lock n’ Load” under angry, dark, chasing, heavy, restless — that is the register.

    The frame is widening. “Round Around” with Nateki is phonk house, aimed at a floor rather than a feed. “MONTAGEM HYSTERIA” with -Prey, pvppet and ToufG steps into Brazilian montagem. What does not change is who makes it: produced, mixed and mastered by one person.

    It started with one track.

    “help urself” spread through fan edits on TikTok and YouTube Shorts. No PR. No budget. 43 million streams on that one track. “X-PIRATA” followed with 2.6 million.

    Norway is here.

    HXI is part of a growing Norwegian phonk scene. His collab with Slowboy and Crazy Mano on “De Norway Com Amor” connected Nordic and Brazilian phonk worldwide.

    Long — for features and one-sheets

    HXI is Christoffer Andersen, an independent producer, DJ and sound designer working out of Oslo, Norway. Since 2021 he has built a catalogue in drift phonk — the fast, bass-heavy strain of a genre that began in Memphis — with bass house and Brazilian phonk running through it. His reach came from creators rather than radio: “help urself” has passed 43.4M+ streams on Spotify — approximate, checked August 2026 — carried largely by the anime, gaming and gym edit communities who used it in their own work. That relationship is deliberate. Two tracks are cleared for creators to use and monetise, and he has releases with NoCopyrightSounds, the platform built on the same principle. He works alone by choice: tracks are written, mixed and mastered by HXI unless a collaborator is credited on the release, and he self-releases except where a release partner is named — which means he owns his masters on his own work. It is an unusual position for an artist at his stream count, and it is the one he intends to keep. HXI is available for sync in games, film, television and creator content, for remixes and collaborations, and for DJ sets and live shows worldwide.

    The bios stay in English on purpose: these are the wordings HXI has cleared, and a translation nobody has checked is not cleared. Translate freely — just do not attribute the translation to him.

    Both figures are approximate and carry the month they were last checked. Please print them that way, and confirm against Spotify for Artists if your piece runs much later.

    Questions

    The things people ask

    Who is HXI?

    HXI is Christoffer Andersen, an independent drift phonk producer, DJ and sound designer from Oslo, Norway, working under that name since 2021. The name is said “hoy”.

    What is drift phonk?

    Phonk began in Memphis in the 1990s, built out of slowed cassette rap and cowbell. Drift phonk is the fast, bass-heavy branch that grew from it online: higher tempo, distorted 808 cowbell, and arrangements cut to sit under a moving image. It takes its name from the drift-car videos it was first edited into.

    Is HXI on NCS?

    Yes. “Lock n’ Load” (September 2024) and “Round Around” with Nateki (23 September 2025) are NoCopyrightSounds releases.

    Can I use HXI’s music in my videos?

    The two NCS tracks are free to use in your own videos, monetised ones included, as long as you credit them the way NCS asks. The exact credit line is in the “Use it” section with a button that copies it. Anything else in the catalogue needs permission first — ask before you publish, not after.

    Is the music made with AI?

    No — not in the music. Every release is written, mixed and mastered by HXI, with collaborators credited on the releases they worked on. Where AI was used is on this page itself: the twelve translations were machine-assisted and are still being checked by native speakers, so the English is the wording HXI stands behind. The site also reserves its content against AI training.

    How do I book HXI?

    Write to booking@hximusic.com. DJ sets, live shows and B2B — based in Oslo, available worldwide.

    I am writing about HXI. Where do I start?

    press@hximusic.com. Bios you can publish without asking anyone are in the Press section, and interviews happen by email, by call, or in person in Oslo.
