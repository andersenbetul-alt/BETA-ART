What “verified at the source” means

Every photograph in the archive was made by a person with a camera. Nothing here is generated, composited or enhanced by a machine — and that is a written guarantee, not a house style. Three records stand behind it, and each one is made before anybody asks for it.

  1. The original file is kept

    Every plate begins as a RAW file from a real camera, archived within a day with a checksum and available for inspection.

  2. The capture record travels with the image

    Camera, lens, exposure, place and time, written at the shoot. Where the equipment supports it, content credentials are signed in camera.

  3. The licence is signed by the maker

    You license directly from the photographer, with a written licence, a proper invoice and a numbered certificate. No intermediary, and no uncertainty about who holds the rights.

If a plate sold as verified is ever shown to have been generated by a machine, the licence fee is refunded in full. A guarantee with no consequence attached is a slogan.
