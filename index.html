How it works

Four steps from "something is wrong" to the right doctor

No referral letters, no waiting rooms, and no having to know which specialist to ask for.

Tell us what is wrong

Type or pick your complaint in your own language. Plain words are fine — no medical terms needed.

We check for anything urgent

A short set of safety questions decides whether this can wait, needs today, or needs emergency care now.

We find the right specialty

Your answers are matched against the specialties that treat them, so you skip the guesswork and the wrong queue.

You leave with your case written up

A summary of everything a doctor asks in the first two minutes — in your language. Book a doctor who is free now, or take it to your own hospital appointment.

The queue problem

Most of that wait was never necessary

People spend a morning in a hospital queue to be told they are in the wrong department. They get sent away to book with someone else, and start again. Meanwhile the person behind them, who did need that department, waited too.

Almost none of that is a medical problem. It is a sorting problem, and it can be solved before anyone leaves the house — by working out where someone belongs and sending them there with their information already in order.

  • Know which department handles your problem before you travel
  • Arrive with your history, medicines and questions written down
  • Find out in two minutes whether this needs a hospital at all
  • Free up the queue for the people who genuinely need to be in it

You do not have to book with us to use this

The symptom check and the summary it produces are free, run entirely on your own device, and are yours to keep. Print it and take it to your own doctor if that is what you need. We would rather you arrived at the right place prepared than paid us for a consultation you did not need.

Why doctors join

Nearly every clinician has watched someone struggle through an appointment in a language they barely speak, and known the consultation was worse for it. If you share a language with a patient, you are often the difference between a useful appointment and a wasted one — and there are far more of those patients than there are of you.

Naviar Care does not replace emergency services

If someone is unconscious, struggling to breathe, bleeding heavily, or you think they are having a heart attack or stroke, call your local emergency number now. Our intake will tell you the same thing the moment it spots a warning sign.
