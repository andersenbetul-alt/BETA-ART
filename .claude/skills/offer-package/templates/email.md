# Warm scheduling email — skeleton

Write it in the candidate's language. No numbers — they belong on the call.

---

**Subject:** Next step at [COMPANY] — a conversation about our offer?

Hi [FIRST NAME],

thank you for a genuinely good conversation — [ONE SPECIFIC MOMENT FROM THE
INTERVIEW] stayed with us, in the best way.

We have decided we would love to have you as [ROLE], and want to walk you
through the offer: the role, the terms, and how the first weeks work. No
decision is needed on the call — we will go through it together, and you get
everything in writing afterwards.

Would [DAY/DATE] at [TIME] work, by video or phone — whichever you prefer?

Best,
[SENDER NAME]
[COMPANY]
