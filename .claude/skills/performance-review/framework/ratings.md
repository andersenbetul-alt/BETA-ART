# Rating scale — DRAFT v0.1, owner approval pending

> Derived 2026-08-25 from the pilot adviser role spec's 90-day success
> criteria (rework rate ≤ 20 %, zero scope breaches, SLA held). Three levels
> on purpose: a pilot with a handful of advisers cannot calibrate five.
> **No review may be finalised against this draft until the owner approves
> it.** The skill quotes these definitions verbatim — edit the words here,
> not in the review.

## Over forventning (Exceeds)

Every delivery in the period passed quality_check on first submission, no
boundary or AI-rule finding of any severity, and at least one improvement
the adviser proposed was adopted into a written scope.

## Som forventet (Meets)

Deliveries pass quality_check with a rework rate at or below 20 %, zero
scope or AI-rule breaches, response and delivery commitments held or
re-negotiated before the deadline.

## Under forventning (Below)

Rework above 20 % across the period, or any single scope, boundary or
AI-rule breach, or commitments missed without flagging in advance. One
breach places the period here regardless of volume — the boundary is the
business.
