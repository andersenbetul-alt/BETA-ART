## Faglig kvalitet (Craft)

Delivers the written scope of the assigned service — the scope documents in
`leveranser/` say what the adviser does and where they stop — on the
committed SLA, with work that passes quality_check. A person checks every
personalised deliverable before it goes out; work that is not good enough
goes back to the person who did it.
*Source: docs/tjenestemodell.md §3 and §6.*

## Grensedisiplin (Boundaries)

Stays inside the refusal list without exception: never interprets a vedtak,
never gives legal, health, tax or residency advice, never contacts an
employer or public body on the customer's behalf, never promises a job or an
interview, never takes payment outside the system, and sees only the file
assigned to them.
*Source: docs/tjenestemodell.md §3 ("What we will not take", career rules,
pilot organisation).*

## AI-disiplin (AI discipline)

Uses AI only on what the customer supplied, treats every AI output as a
draft until read through, never lets invented experience, education, results
or references survive into a deliverable, and never puts sensitive data into
an open AI tool.
*Source: docs/tjenestemodell.md §3 ("The career rules").*

## Pålitelighet (Reliability)

Responds to assigned-case communication within one business day, holds the
delivery dates given to the customer, and flags early — before the deadline,
not after — when a case will slip or does not fit.
*Source: pilot adviser role spec, 90-day criteria 3–4.*

## Forbedring (Improvement)

Feeds what the cases teach back into the written scopes: concrete proposals,
not complaints. The scope documents are what make two advisers deliver the
same service; they only stay true if the people delivering them correct them.
*Source: pilot adviser role spec, 90-day criterion 5.*
