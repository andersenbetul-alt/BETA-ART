Where you can practise

Medical licensing follows the patient, not the doctor. You can consult with patients anywhere in the world, but you may only diagnose and prescribe where you hold a licence. We label every session so both you and the patient know which it is before it starts.

Read how we handle this

Working with health facilities

Some countries — Türkiye among them — require remote consultations to be delivered through a licensed health facility rather than by an individual doctor. If that applies where you are, we will tell you during verification and connect you to a partner facility.
