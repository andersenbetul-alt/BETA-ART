Legal & regulatory

Last updated 30 August 2026. This page describes how we operate, what we can and cannot do, and how we handle your information.

Scope of service

Naviar Care is a platform that connects patients with licensed medical professionals for remote consultations. The service provides routing assistance (working out which specialty fits a described symptom), real-time booking of available clinicians, and interpretation services.

Naviar Care is a health guidance service. It is not a substitute for in-person examination, emergency services, or a primary care relationship. The symptom checker produces a routing suggestion, not a diagnosis.

Medical licensing

How we verify clinicians

Every clinician listed on Naviar Care is verified against the register of the issuing authority before their profile is made visible. We check:

  • Active registration with the issuing body
  • Scope of practice matches the specialty declared
  • Professional indemnity insurance covering telemedicine
  • Two professional referees, contacted directly

Where a doctor can diagnose and prescribe

Medical licensing follows the patient, not the doctor. A clinician may diagnose and prescribe only in the countries where they hold an active licence. Where a clinician is not licensed in the patient's country, the session is labelled a second opinion — the clinician can discuss symptoms and provide information but cannot issue a diagnosis, prescription or referral in that jurisdiction.

This is shown clearly on the booking screen before a patient confirms. The clinician sees the same label.

Health facility requirements

Some jurisdictions — Türkiye among them — require remote medical consultations to be delivered through a licensed health facility rather than by an individual practitioner. Where this applies, we connect the clinician to a partner facility and the consultation is conducted under that facility's licence. We identify these cases during verification and inform the clinician before their profile goes live.

Emergency disclaimer

Naviar Care is not an emergency service. The platform cannot dispatch emergency responders, contact emergency services, or monitor a patient's condition in real time.

Our symptom intake checks for warning signs and, when it identifies one, instructs the user to stop and contact their local emergency number. This check is not exhaustive and does not replace clinical judgement.

If you or someone near you is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number now.

Data and privacy

Symptom check

The symptom checker runs entirely in your browser. The answers you enter are not transmitted to our servers while you are completing the check. If you proceed to book, the summary you approve is sent to the clinician you select — nothing is shared without that step.

Consultation data

Booking details are transmitted to and stored by Naviar Care for the duration of the consultation and for a period afterwards to allow follow-up. Clinicians access only what they need to conduct the consultation. Interpreters work under the same confidentiality obligations as clinicians.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your health information with advertisers
  • We do not use your consultation data to train AI models without your explicit consent
  • We do not require you to create an account to use the symptom checker

Your records

The summary produced by your symptom check belongs to you. You can print it, save it, or take it to another healthcare provider — there is no lock-in. If you booked through us, your consultation notes are available to you on request.

We retain booking records for a minimum period required by applicable health data regulations in the patient's country and in Norway, where Naviar Care is based. After that period, records are deleted or anonymised.

To request a copy of your data or to ask for deletion, contact us at the contact form.

Liability

Naviar Care provides a platform connecting patients and clinicians. Clinical responsibility rests with the clinician conducting the consultation. Naviar Care is not liable for clinical decisions made by listed practitioners.

The symptom checker is an automated routing tool. It does not provide medical advice and should not be relied on as a substitute for clinical assessment.

Demo notice

This site is a working demonstration of the Naviar Care service. The clinician roster, availability numbers, and consultation prices shown are sample data. No real consultation is booked and no payment is processed through this version of the site.
