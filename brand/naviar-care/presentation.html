The four directions

Same palette and wordmark across all four, so the only variable you are judging is the mark.

A Wayfinder

Naviar carries navigate inside it. A compass ring, left open at the lower right so it reads as an arm around something rather than a closed dial, with the needle pointing forward. The promise is guidance: families rarely know what care they need until someone walks them through it.

  • Reads asDirection, expertise, someone who knows the route
  • StrengthTies straight to the name — the only direction that does
  • RiskCompasses are crowded territory in consulting and finance; the needle blurs below about 24 px
  • Best onWebsite, brochures, van livery — anywhere it is drawn large

B Cradle

Two open arcs holding a single figure. Depending on the moment it reads as cupped hands, a pair of shoulders, or a bed — all of them true. Nothing encloses the figure: the arcs support from below and leave the top open, which is the difference between care and custody, and it is the argument this brand should be making.

  • Reads asSupport, dignity, a person still at the centre
  • StrengthWarmest and most human of the four without a single heart in sight
  • RiskThe two arcs start to merge under 20 px; needs the wordmark to be unambiguous on first meeting
  • Best onUniforms, signage, care-plan documents, social profiles

Recommended. It says the most about how the service behaves, and it is the one a family would remember from a doorstep visit.

C Monogram

An N built from three strokes where the diagonal leans on both uprights and carries the accent colour. Support drawn as structure rather than sentiment. It is the most practical of the four: round caps keep it friendly, and the counters stay open at any size.

  • Reads asModern, organised, quietly confident
  • StrengthSurvives further down than the rest — app icon, favicon, embroidery, stamped metal
  • RiskSays nothing about care on its own; it needs the wordmark beside it to carry meaning
  • Best onApp icon, favicon, invoice stationery, badge lanyards

Strong companion to B: run the cradle as the brand mark and the N wherever the artwork shrinks below 20 px.

D Hearth

A house with a heart inside it. No interpretation required — a person scanning a leaflet in a hospital corridor knows what this company does in under a second. That legibility is the whole case for it, and it is not a small case.

  • Reads asCare at home, warmth, familiarity
  • StrengthInstantly understood by every audience, including the least design-literate
  • RiskThe most expected mark in this category; hard to own, and near-identical marks already exist locally
  • Best onLeaflets and referral material aimed at first-time enquirers

Usage rules

Clear space

Keep free space equal to the mark's stroke width × 3 on all four sides of the lockup.

Minimum size

Mark 20 px / 7 mm. Full lockup 120 px / 32 mm — below that, use the mark alone.

On photography

Reversed artwork only, over a dark area or a Night overlay at 55% or more.

Never

Recolour the mark outside the palette, add a shadow or outline, stretch it, or set the wordmark in another face.
