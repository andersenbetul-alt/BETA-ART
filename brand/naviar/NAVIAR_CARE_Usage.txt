NAVIAR CARE — Brand Usage Guidelines

Date: 2026-08-30
Scope: NAVIAR CARE descriptor lockup and all NAVIAR CARE branded materials

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. MASTER COLOURS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Midnight Navy  #0A1628   Primary — backgrounds, type on light
  Premium Gold   #D4AF37   Structural accent only (monogram bar)
  Off White      #F5F6F8   Type on dark, reverse backgrounds
  Graphite       #1E1E1E   Secondary neutral (monochrome print)

  Note: Gold achieves only 2.10:1 contrast on white — it fails
  WCAG 2.1 AA for text and load-bearing graphics on light backgrounds.
  Gold is a structural accent, not a type colour.
  On light backgrounds: descriptor and body type must use Midnight Navy.
  On dark (navy) backgrounds: type and descriptor may use Off White.

  Accent Cyan #00B2E3 is a data/UI colour only. It must never appear
  in the master NAVIAR CARE mark or on branded stationery.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2. MINIMUM SIZES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Full lockup (monogram + NAVIAR + CARE descriptor):
    Digital  160 px  minimum width
    Print     35 mm  minimum width

  Wordmark only (NAVIAR, no descriptor):
    Digital   96 px  minimum width
    Print     24 mm  minimum width

  These are hard minima. Below these sizes readability of
  the CARE descriptor and monogram detail is not guaranteed.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3. CLEAR SPACE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Lockup (horizontal / stacked):  ≥ 0.30H on all sides
    where H = cap height of the NAVIAR wordmark (100 units)

  Monogram alone:  ≥ 1 ribbon width (150 units at master scale)
    on all sides

  Nothing — text, image, border, fold — may enter the clear space.

4. DESCRIPTOR GOVERNANCE — CARE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  4.1  Cap height
       CARE descriptor cap height = 27% of NAVIAR wordmark cap height.
       Acceptable range: 24–30%.

  4.2  Colour
       Light background: Midnight Navy (#0A1628).
       Dark / navy background: Off White (#F5F6F8).
       Gold is not permitted as descriptor colour on any background.

  4.3  Position
       Centred below the NAVIAR wordmark, aligned to wordmark width.
       Horizontal separation from wordmark baseline: 20 units.

  4.4  Font reference
       Poppins weight 500, or a construction reference for outlined
       masters. The descriptor in production artwork must be outlined
       vector paths — live text is not permitted in the filing master.

  4.5  Architectural status
       CARE is not in the approved master architecture as of 2026-08-30.
       Use of this descriptor requires explicit business approval and
       completion of a separate Nice class 44 trademark search.
       Until both are granted, the CARE lockup file is named
       naviar-care-PENDING-APPROVAL.svg and must not be used in
       production, publications, or trademark filings.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5. DO NOT LIST
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  × Gradients, bevels, shadows, glows, metal textures, 3D effects
  × Raster masks overlaid on the master mark
  × Permanently embedding a tagline inside the wordmark artwork
  × Redrawing the wordmark geometry (use the master SVG output only)
  × Country-specific or language-specific variants
  × Translating, mirroring, or rotating the NAVIAR wordmark
  × Using Gold as body text or descriptive label on light backgrounds
  × Using Accent Cyan in the master mark or branded stationery
  × Open-R letterform (the closed bowl + diagonal leg is mandatory)
  × Dot-A letterform (dots are retired; plain triangular A is master)
  × Combining the CARE descriptor with a tagline in the same lockup

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6. TRADEMARK STATUS AND RISK
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  The NAVIAR CARE mark carries the same pre-filing risk as the
  NAVIAR master mark (see docs/naviar/NAVIAR-LOGO-KARAR.md §5).

  Additional risk specific to CARE:
  — "CARE" is descriptive for health/care services under SMK art. 5/1-c
    and EUTMR art. 7(1)(c); it does not generate exclusive rights on its
    own. Protection attaches to the NAVIAR element.
  — Nice class 44 (medical and care services) must be searched before
    any use if the service is health or care related.
  — Sector-specific advertising restrictions for health services
    must be verified once the service definition is final.
  — A disclaimer for CARE is expected upon registration.

  Searches required before production use:
    NAVIAR CARE — exact + phonetic/spelling variants
    Patentstyret (Norway) · EUIPO/TMview (EU) · WIPO GBD (international)
    Nice classes: 35, 41, 42, 9, 44

  Professional registry clearance and legal advice are required
  before trademark filing, signage, and public rollout.
