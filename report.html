Report something unlawful.

The form

The four things asked for below are what Article 16 of the Digital Services Act (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065) says a notice should contain. Anything you add beyond them helps.

Copy it from the address bar. If it is a photograph, add the plate number — for example 2026.0148.

What happens next

  • Acknowledged by email, without undue delay.
  • Read by a person. No automated system decides whether something comes down. That is slower, and it is the only way an answer can carry a reason.
  • Decided in a timely, diligent and non-arbitrary way, and you are told the outcome with the reasons for it.
  • Acted on where the notice is well founded: the content comes down, and if it is a photograph, the plate is withdrawn from sale and any live licence is refunded in full.
  • The other side is told what was decided and why, and can dispute it.

If you disagree with the outcome, reply and say why — it will be reconsidered. You may also take the matter to an out-of-court dispute settlement body or to a court. Nothing on this page limits that or is a condition of it.

If it is your own image, you do not need a legal argument

Write to hallo@beta-art.com and say you withdraw your consent. Publication and licensing stop. What has already been licensed to a buyer cannot always be recalled — we will tell you exactly what is out and what we have done about it, rather than promise more than we can deliver.

The rules this page enforces are set out in section 12 of the legal notice, and what we promise to do with a notice is in section 13.

The consent form itself is here, and it is worth reading even if you never sign one: it says, point by point, what is and is not being asked for.

Misuse

A notice sent in bad faith — to remove work that is not yours, or to silence something lawful — wastes the time that belongs to real notices. Repeated bad-faith notices will be refused, and we will say why. That is the only sanction here, and it is enough.

Human approved

Nothing is published here until a person has seen it and approved it.
