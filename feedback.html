How did it go?

We ask both sides after every consultation. Patient ratings are what other patients see; the doctor's answers tell us whether our routing sent them the right person.

Who is filling this in?

Why we ask both sides

A patient can tell whether they felt heard and understood. Only the doctor can tell whether the specialty we routed them to was the right one. We need both to know whether this is working.

How ratings are shown

A doctor's rating is a simple mean of scores from patients who actually completed a consultation with them. We do not filter out low scores, we do not reorder by how flattering a review is, and nobody can pay to change their rating.

If something went wrong

Feedback is not a complaints channel. If you were harmed, treated badly, or believe a clinician acted improperly, write to us directly and a person will handle it.
