Privacy

What this site stores

Almost nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixel and no cookie. This page says exactly what happens, because a short honest list is worth more than a long one nobody reads.

Collected automatically

Nothing. The site is a set of static files. It runs no analytics and sets no cookies. The fonts are served from this domain, so no request goes to a font provider either.

Your language choice

When you pick a language, the code for it (“tr”, “ar”, and so on) is saved in your browser under “hxi-lang”, so the next visit opens in the same language. It stays on your device, it identifies nobody, and clearing your browser data removes it.

If you write or sign up

The signup form and every contact button open your own mail app. Your address reaches HXI the same way any email does, and it is used to send what you asked for — releases, drops, or an answer to your message. It is not sold, rented or passed on. Reply to any message and you are off the list.

Video and music players

YouTube and Spotify players load only after you press play. Until you do, neither company knows you are here. Once you press play, the player is delivered by them and they receive your IP address and the usual data their own policies describe. YouTube is loaded through youtube-nocookie.com.

Hosting

The site is served as static files by its host, which keeps ordinary server logs — the address that requested a file, and when. HXI does not read or receive those logs.

Your rights

If you are in the EU or the EEA you can ask what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to its use. In practice that means the email address you sent, since there is nothing else. Write and it is done.
