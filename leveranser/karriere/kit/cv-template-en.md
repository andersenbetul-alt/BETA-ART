# CV template — English

Copy everything below the line. Replace anything in `[square brackets]`, and
delete what does not apply to you. An empty heading is worse than no heading.

**The rule for the whole template:** every line must be true of you. Where you
do not have a number, rewrite the line — do not guess.

A note on the Norwegian market: leave out your date of birth, marital status,
photo and national ID number unless the advert asks. Two pages maximum.

### Before you send it

1. Read the advert. Mark the five most important requirements.
2. Make sure all five appear in the CV, in the advert's own words.
3. Cut back anything not relevant to this particular job.
4. Run `gratis/cv-sjekkliste.md`.
5. Save as PDF: `First-Last-CV.pdf`.
