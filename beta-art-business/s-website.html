Who this is for

  • Small and mid-sized companies whose site was built once and abandoned
  • Companies whose site is slow, unreadable on a phone, or both
  • Organisations that need to meet accessibility obligations

The problem

What usually goes wrong

Most company websites fail for one of three reasons: nobody can update them without calling an agency, they take four seconds to load on a phone, or they explain the company rather than the offer. The visitor leaves before the argument starts.

The solution

What we do instead

A site built around what you sell, in a structure your team can maintain. Fast because it is not carrying six analytics scripts. Accessible because that is a legal obligation in Norway and the EEA, not a nice-to-have. Written so a visitor understands the offer in the first screen.

FAQ

Questions asked about this service

Which platform?

Whichever you can maintain. Often static or a light CMS. We do not push a platform that pays us a commission.

Do we own it?

Yes — source, domain, hosting and accounts transfer to you on full payment.

What about accessibility?

Built in, not added. Keyboard, contrast, semantic structure and reduced-motion support are part of the delivery.
