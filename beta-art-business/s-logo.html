The problem

What usually goes wrong

Most small companies own a logo they cannot use: one file, one colour, wrong format, no version that works on a dark background or at 16 pixels. Every printer, platform and partner asks for something the company does not have.

The solution

What we do instead

A mark designed to work at every size it will actually appear at, delivered in every format you will be asked for, with a short guide covering the four rules that keep it consistent.
