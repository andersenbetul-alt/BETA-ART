The problem

What usually goes wrong

Administration is not difficult; it is relentless. Sorting an inbox, drafting the same three letters, entering an order into two systems, remembering that an invoice is nine days overdue. None of it needs judgement, all of it needs doing, and it is almost always done by the person whose time is worth the most.

The solution

What we do instead

An assistant that takes the routine layer: the inbox triaged by what needs a decision and what does not, the standard documents drafted from your own templates, data entered once and carried across, and the quiet chases sent on time. Everything it produces is a draft until a person approves it, so nothing leaves the building unread.

FAQ

Questions asked about this service

Can it send email as me?

Only what you put on the approved list. Everything else waits as a draft.

What about confidential documents?

You name the folders and categories it may never touch, and it cannot reach them. Access is scoped, logged and revocable.

How do we know it is saving time?

The time audit at the start gives the baseline, and the monthly summary reports against it. If the hours do not appear, the answer is to stop, not to add more automation.
