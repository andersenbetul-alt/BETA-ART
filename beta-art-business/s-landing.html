The problem

What usually goes wrong

Paid traffic sent to a homepage converts badly, because a homepage is built to serve everyone and a visitor from an advert wants one thing. Every extra link on that page is an exit.

The solution

What we do instead

A single page with a single action. The advert's promise is repeated in the first line, the objections are answered in the order people raise them, and there is exactly one thing to do. Measured, so you know whether it worked.

How it works

  2. 02

  4. 04
