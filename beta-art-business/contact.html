What to expect

Response times, honestly

  • Email — same working day, usually within a few hours.
  • A brief sent through the quote flow — a written scope, a fixed price and a delivery date within 48 hours.
  • A call request — confirmed within a day, held within the week.
  • A privacy or rights request — acknowledged immediately, answered within 30 days as the law requires, usually much sooner.
  • Outside working hours — read, not answered. Nothing here is urgent enough to pretend otherwise.

When we say no

We would rather decline than quote badly

If the project needs a team we do not have, if the budget and the scope cannot both be true, or if we think another supplier is a better fit, we will say so in the first reply and say why. That is not a soft rejection — it is the most useful thing we can give you in 48 hours if the answer is no.
