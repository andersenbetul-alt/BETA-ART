    FAQ

    Questions people ask first.

    How does it actually work?

    You describe the problem, we send a written scope with a fixed price and a date, and on approval we build it. Seven steps, listed above, the same for every project.

    Is the work done by AI or by a person?

    A person is responsible for every delivery and signs off on it. AI is used where it genuinely helps — drafting, sorting, research — and never as a substitute for judgement. We will tell you which parts were machine-assisted if you ask.

    How many revisions do I get?

    Two rounds in Starter, unlimited within the agreed term on Professional and Premium. Additional rounds are quoted before they begin.

    How long does it take?

    Starter deliverables in 5–10 working days. A full website is typically 4–8 weeks depending on content. The date is written into the scope, not estimated afterwards.

    Are my files safe?

    Project material is kept in access-controlled storage, never sold, never passed to advertisers, and never used to train models. You can ask for deletion at any time after handover.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes. Before work starts, at no cost. Once started, you pay for the stage that has been completed and receive everything produced up to that point. Consumers in Norway and the EEA also have a 14-day right of withdrawal — see the refunds section.

    What is the difference between Private and Business?

    Private serves one person and their career or personal presence. Business serves an organisation and includes commercial rights, invoicing to a company and, where needed, a framework agreement.

    Which privacy laws do you follow?

    All of them that can apply to us: the GDPR and the Norwegian personopplysningsloven first, and then the UK GDPR, Swiss FADP, Turkish KVKK, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA and Québec Law 25, POPIA, PDPA, APPI, PIPA, PIPL, India’s DPDP Act and the Australian Privacy Act. The full list and your rights under each are in the privacy section.
