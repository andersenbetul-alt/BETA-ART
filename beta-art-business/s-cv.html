Who this is for

  • People applying in Norway or the EEA who are getting no replies
  • Career changers whose experience does not map neatly onto the job title
  • Anyone whose CV has grown by accretion over ten years

The problem

What usually goes wrong

Most CVs are rejected before a human sees them. Applicant-tracking systems parse the file, look for the terms in the advert, and rank what they find. A beautiful two-column layout with the job titles in a sidebar often scores zero — not because the candidate is weak, but because the file could not be read.

The solution

What we do instead

The CV is rebuilt in a structure the parser can read and a person wants to read: one column, real headings, results before responsibilities, and the vocabulary of the adverts you are actually answering. You get the master document plus a short guide for tailoring it per application.

How it works

The steps, in order

  1. 01

    Send what you have

    Current CV, two adverts you would apply to, and anything you are proud of that is missing from the page.

  2. 02

    We map it

    Your experience against what those adverts screen for — gaps named honestly, not papered over.

  3. 03

    Rebuild

    One column, results first, a number in at least half the lines.

  4. 04

    Tailoring guide

    Which three lines to change per application, and which never to touch.

  5. 05

    Two revisions

    One consolidated set of comments each round.

Deliverables

What you actually receive

  • Master CV as .docx and PDF, named firstname-lastname-cv.pdf
  • Application letter template with the three variable blocks marked
  • A one-page tailoring guide
  • Keyword list drawn from real adverts in your field

FAQ

Questions asked about this service

Will you invent experience I do not have?

No. We reframe what is true and name the gaps so you can answer them in an interview instead of being caught by them.

Do I need a photo?

In Norway, usually not. We advise per market and supply both versions if you want the choice.
