4 · AI and training data

Beta Art exists partly because the difference between human and machine authorship has stopped being obvious. Our position is explicit.

Reservation of rights

All content on this site, on the archive and in the journal is expressly reserved against text and data mining under Article 4(3) of Directive (EU) 2019/790 and the corresponding provision of åndsverkloven. This is a machine-readable reservation, reinforced by the noai, noimageai meta directive on every page and by explicit disallow rules for AI crawlers in robots.txt, including GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Applebot-Extended, PerplexityBot, Amazonbot, Diffbot, Omgilibot, FacebookBot and cohere-ai.

No licence to use Beta Art material as training data, as retrieval material for a generative system, or as input to synthetic media is granted, offered or implied — including to operators who ignore the reservation. Ignoring it does not create a licence; it creates a claim.

How we use AI in the work

  • A person is responsible for every delivery and approves it before it leaves the studio.
  • AI is used for drafting, structuring, research and repetitive processing. It is never presented as a photographic original, and it is never the final judgement.
  • We will tell you, on request, which parts of your delivery were machine-assisted.
  • Your project material is never submitted to a model that trains on it. Where a service must be used, the training-excluded or enterprise mode is used.
  • Photographic work sold through the Beta Art archive is human-captured only. That is the entire point of the archive, and it is verified before a work is accessioned.

6 · Refunds and withdrawal

Consumers — 14-day right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer in Norway or the EEA, you have 14 days from the day the agreement is concluded to withdraw from it, under the Norwegian Angrerettloven and Directive 2011/83/EU. You do not have to give a reason. Write to hallo@beta-art.com, or use the standard withdrawal form; we confirm receipt in writing and refund within 14 days using the same payment method you used.

Two exceptions, both of which we will make explicit before you agree to them:

  • If you ask us to start before the 14 days expire, and the service is fully performed within that period, the right of withdrawal ends on completion.
  • If you withdraw after work has started at your request, you pay a proportionate amount for what was delivered up to that point — and you receive it.
  • Goods and services made to your specification and clearly personalised (a bespoke logo, a written CV, a custom photographic commission) are exempt from withdrawal once production has begun with your consent.

Business customers

Cancellation before work starts is free. After work starts, the completed stage is invoiced and everything produced is handed over. Retainers run monthly and can be ended with 30 days' written notice.

If something is wrong

Tell us. A defect in our work is fixed at our cost. If it cannot be fixed within a reasonable time, you get a proportionate price reduction or a refund of the affected part. Consumers keep the full statutory complaint period (reklamasjonsrett) regardless of anything written here.

What is collected, and why

  • What you type into a form — name, email, the description of your project, budget and deadline. Purpose: to answer you and prepare a quote. Basis: your consent and the steps taken at your request before a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and (b)).

8 · Cookies

This site sets no tracking cookies, no advertising cookies and no third-party analytics cookies. It uses one entry in your browser's local storage to remember that you dismissed the notice, and one for a language choice if you make one. Both are strictly functional, stay on your device, and can be cleared at any time from your browser settings.

Fonts are requested from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts that service and it sees your IP address. If you prefer to avoid that entirely, a self-hosted font build is available on request; the site remains fully readable with fonts blocked.

9 · Accessibility

Beta Art aims at WCAG 2.2 level AA across all three sites, in line with the Norwegian likestillings- og diskrimineringsloven §18 and forskrift om universell utforming av IKT, and with the European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882).

  • Every page works with keyboard alone, with visible focus indicators.
  • Semantic headings, landmarks, labelled form fields and live regions for status messages.
  • Text contrast meets or exceeds 4.5:1; interface contrast 3:1.
  • Motion is removed entirely when the operating system requests reduced motion.
  • No information is carried by colour alone; nothing depends on hover.
  • Text resizes to 200% without loss of content or function.

If any part of this site blocks you, write to hallo@beta-art.com. We treat it as a defect, fix it, and give you the information another way in the meantime.

10 · Enforcement

Rights that are never enforced stop being rights. Beta Art enforces.

  • Unlicensed use of a photographic work, a text, the mark or the page design is invoiced at three times the standard licence fee for the equivalent use, plus the cost of investigation — the established measure for unauthorised use in Nordic practice.
  • Takedowns are issued to the host, the registrar, the CDN, the platform and the search engine simultaneously, under the DMCA in the United States, the DSA in the EU, and the equivalent notice procedure elsewhere.
  • Cloned sites and copied concepts are pursued as copyright infringement of the design and copy, and, where the mark or get-up is involved, as trade-mark infringement and unfair competition under markedsføringsloven §§25 and 30.
  • Evidence is preserved routinely: dated source archives, RAW originals with checksums, C2PA content credentials on published imagery, and timestamped deployment records.
  • Jurisdiction: Norwegian law, Stavanger tingrett, without prejudice to a consumer's right to their home forum or to our right to seek injunctive relief wherever the infringement occurs.

If you believe your own rights have been affected by something published here, write to hallo@beta-art.com. We answer every notice, and we remove anything that should not be up.
