The problem

What usually goes wrong

A wall of thumbnails shows that you produced things. It does not show that you can be trusted with a budget. The buyer is trying to work out how you think, and a grid of finished images does not tell them.

The solution

What we do instead

Each project is written as a short case: the problem the client had, what you actually did, and what came of it. The images support the argument rather than being the argument. Three strong cases beat twelve thin ones, and we will say so if you send twelve.

How it works

The steps, in order

  1. 01

    Selection

    Which projects to show, and which to leave out.

  2. 02

    Case interviews

    One call per project to get the problem and the result out of you.

  3. 03

    Writing

    Each case in problem / work / result form.

  4. 04

    Build

    Responsive gallery with the cases as the spine.

  5. 05

    Two revisions

Usually three to five. More dilutes.
