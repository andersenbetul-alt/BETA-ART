The problem

What usually goes wrong

A missed call is rarely a customer who calls back. They call the next name on the list. Most small companies know this and still miss a third of their calls, because the person who could answer is holding a drill, a client, or a child. The usual fix is an answering service that reads a script and takes a message — which is a slower way of not answering.

The solution

What we do instead

A receptionist that is on duty every hour of the year. It answers the phone and the message channels in your voice, knows your opening hours, prices and services, books straight into your calendar, and hands anything it cannot settle to a named person with the whole conversation attached. It never puts anyone on hold and never has a bad morning.

FAQ

Questions asked about this service

Will callers know it is not a person?

Yes — it says so in the greeting. Pretending otherwise is both wrong and, for calls, legally risky in several markets.

What happens when it does not know?

It says so, takes the details, and passes the whole conversation to the person you named. It is not allowed to guess about prices or availability.

Who owns the recordings and transcripts?

You do. Retention is set by you, deletion is possible on request, and the lawful basis is recorded — the detail is in our legal notice.
