Preview

What the client area will look like.

This is a working preview with sample data, not a login. There is no account, no server behind it and nothing is stored — the numbers below are invented and labelled as such. We would rather show you the design honestly than put a login box on a door with no room behind it.

Why it is not live yet. A real client area holds contracts, invoices and project files. That needs a backend with authentication, encrypted storage, audit logging and a documented deletion process — which is a build in its own right, and it will ship when it can meet the privacy standard the rest of this studio already commits to. Until then, projects run over email and a review link, and that works.
