# HXI — brand book

One page on purpose. A rule a stranger cannot apply is not a rule.

## What this is

**HXI is a Norwegian phonk producer.** Not a label, not a studio, not a brand — one person
in Oslo who makes, mixes and masters the record.

The sound: drift phonk with the Memphis nostalgia taken out. What is left is architectural —
sub-bass you feel before you hear it, glitch-heavy design, rhythm cut to lock with a moving
image. NCS files "Lock n' Load" under angry, dark, chasing, heavy, restless. That is the
register, and it is the register the words should match.

## Who it is for

The people who found the music themselves: the anime and gaming edit communities that carried
"help urself" to 43 million streams without anyone paying for it, and the producers and
listeners around them.

Also served, in this order and no other: press, and promoters.

**Not for:** brands looking for a sync catalogue. That audience was deliberately removed from
the site and stays removed. A page that speaks to buyers stops speaking to listeners.

## Voice

Three rules and two nevers.

1. **Say the number, not the adjective.** Not "one of the most-streamed phonk producers in
   Northern Europe" — nothing ranks that — but "43,394,947 streams, more than every other
   release combined".
   The fact does the praise.
2. **The first sentence answers the question.** No throat-clearing, no "in today's landscape".
3. **Short sentences carry the weight.** Long ones are allowed when the thought needs them —
   but not two in a row.

**Never** open with a question.
**Never** claim something the sources cannot back — see `docs/RESEARCH.md`. An unverified
credit is removed, not softened.

## Proof we are allowed to use

Verified, with sources in `docs/RESEARCH.md`: 43,394,947 Spotify streams on "help urself" ·
the Fast & Furious credit, stated precisely as *WORTH NOTHING (Aggressive Drift Phonk
Version)*, Drift Tape/Phonk Vol 1, with TWISTED · two NCS releases with their dates · the
collaborator list.

Also verified, August 2026: the BODYCAM soundtrack — two tracks, *Never Phased* (with Lurk
and Scythermane) and *KRASH* (with -Prey, March 2024) — and the twenty-release archive in
the Music section, tabulated in `docs/RESEARCH.md`.

Not currently usable: 251,000 monthly listeners as a *current* figure (it moves weekly; the
page rounds to 251K and only the artist can confirm today's number), the TRXPICAL alias
(HXI's own documents name it, nothing public links the two), and the GramArt confirmation of
name and master ownership as a written fact (it is verbal, by the artist's own note — the
page states ownership, it does not cite GramArt).

Industry statistics are never ours. "Major labels take 70%" says nothing about HXI and was
removed for that reason.

## Names

`HXI` — always uppercase, never Hxi.
`Christoffer Andersen` — the person. Used in press contexts, not as a second brand.
Track and release titles keep their own casing exactly: `help urself`, `X-PIRATA`,
`MONTAGEM HYSTERIA`, `Lock n' Load`, `Round Around`. Never title-case them.
Genre words stay lowercase: phonk, drift phonk, phonk house, montagem.

One accent. If something needs to stand out and the accent is already spent nearby, the
answer is space, not a second colour. Nothing below 10px. No gradients as decoration.

## Six rules from HXI's own PR principles

These came from HXI, in `HXI_PR_Kommunikasjonsprinsipper.md`, and they govern every language.
They overlap with Voice above; where they are stricter, they win.

1. **No absolute that one counterexample would break.** Not "zero paid promotion", not "never",
   not "no outside engineers". Say what *is* true: "grew organically", "written and produced
   in-house".
2. **Fact before adjective.** "Heavy sub-bass", not "architectural sound design". A subjective
   grade is unattributable; a number explains itself.
3. **As short as precision allows.** Two sentences a paragraph where possible. If a detail
   would not change what a fan, a sync buyer or a journalist decides, cut it.
4. **No promise about the future.** Not "will respond within 48 hours", not "guaranteed", not
   "announced soon". "We aim to", "typically", or say nothing.
5. **No rights claim without a confirmed basis.** Ownership, naming and licensing statements
   go on the page only where they are confirmed. Where something is unclear, it is left out —
   not hedged, not hinted at.
6. **Credit by the right category.** A collaboration is not a solo placement; a remix is not a
   sync. Get the category right before the number.

Before any copy change, five questions: could one contrary event prove this false · is there
an adjective here that a number would replace · can the sentence be cut in two · are we
promising a future we do not control · is every named credit in the right category. A "maybe"
on any of them means rewrite or remove.

## How to check a piece against this

Read `docs/BRAND.md`, then ask in order: does it say what HXI is in one line · is every claim
in the proof list · does it open with the answer · is the accent spent once · are the titles
cased correctly. Anything this page does not cover is not a violation — it is a gap, and the
gap is worth writing down rather than guessing at.
