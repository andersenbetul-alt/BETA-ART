Getting everyone to the right doctor, faster

Naviar Care exists to shorten the distance between "something is wrong" and someone qualified to help — whoever you are and whatever language you speak.

The problem we are working on

Most people who are seriously unwell do not go straight to the specialist who can treat them. They wait, because getting an appointment is hard. They guess, because nobody has told them which specialty handles their symptom. Or they stay silent, because explaining what hurts in a language they are still learning feels impossible.

Each of those delays makes the eventual treatment harder. Naviar Care removes all three: a doctor within minutes, a routing step that works out the specialty for you, and a consultation held in your own language.

What we hold to

Distance should not decide outcomes

The difference between a treatable problem and a serious one is often just how long it took to reach someone who knew what to look for.

Language is a clinical safety issue

A mistranslated symptom is a misrouted patient. We treat interpretation as part of the medicine, not an optional extra.

Tell people the truth about urgency

Both over- and under-warning are harmful. Our intake is tuned to say 'this can wait' when it can, so that 'go now' still means something.

Nobody should feel like a burden

No queue-jumping, no having to justify why you called, no explaining your symptoms to four people before reaching the right one.

How a consultation actually runs

You describe the problem in your own words

In your own language, with no medical vocabulary required. You can pick from a list if typing is easier.

We check for danger signs before anything else

If your answers suggest an emergency, we stop and say so instead of offering you an appointment.

We match your description to a specialty

Your symptoms, age and how long it has lasted are weighed against the specialties that treat them, and we show you the ranking rather than hiding it.

You pick a doctor who is free and who understands you

Availability and language are shown together, because a doctor you cannot talk to is not really available.

You keep the notes

The summary, advice and any referral are written in your language and stay with you for the next appointment.

Safety and privacy

We are not an emergency service

Naviar Care cannot send an ambulance. For chest pain with sweating, trouble breathing, heavy bleeding, a drooping face, a seizure, or someone who cannot be woken, call your local emergency number immediately.

What the symptom check is and is not

It is a routing tool. It reads your description and works out which specialty treats it and how soon you should be seen. It does not diagnose, it does not rule anything out, and it is not a substitute for examination. When it is uncertain it sends you to a generalist rather than guessing.

What we do with your information

  • The symptom check runs in your browser; nothing is transmitted while you answer
  • Booking details are limited to what the clinician needs to hold the consultation
  • Interpreters are bound by the same confidentiality duty as the clinician
  • Your consultation notes belong to you and you can take them elsewhere

This site is a working demonstration of the Naviar Care service. The clinician roster and availability shown are sample data, and no real consultation is booked.

Frequently asked questions

Who am I actually talking to?

Licensed doctors and, for mental health, licensed psychologists. Every clinician's specialty, languages and years in practice are shown before you book.

What does a consultation cost?

Pricing depends on the specialty and your country. The price is always shown on the booking screen before you confirm — there is never a charge you did not see first.

Can I get a prescription or a sick note?

Often yes, depending on what the law allows where you are. The doctor will tell you during the consultation what they can and cannot issue in your country.

Is the symptom checker a diagnosis?

No. It decides which specialist fits your description and how urgently you should be seen. Only the doctor you speak to can diagnose you.

What happens to what I type?

The symptom check runs entirely in your browser. Nothing leaves your device unless you go on to book, and then only what is needed for the consultation.

What if no doctor speaks my language?

A trained medical interpreter joins the call. They interpret clinical language accurately, so you are never relying on a relative or a translation app to explain a diagnosis.

What if it turns out to be an emergency?

The intake checks for warning signs first. If it spots one it stops and tells you to contact emergency services rather than booking you an appointment.

Do I have to explain everything again next time?

No. Your summary and any referral carry over, so the next clinician starts with your history rather than a blank page.
