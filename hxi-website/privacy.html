Privacy

This notice describes how this site handles inquiries.

What the site collects

If you submit an inquiry, Sync request or Booking request, the form opens your default mail client with the data pre-filled. The data is sent directly from your mail client to the HXI contact address. No inquiry data is stored by this website or any third-party service.

Why it is used

Inquiry data is used to review and respond to professional, booking, press, collaboration, rights or licensing requests.

Third-party media

Spotify is contacted only after you choose to load the player. Google Fonts are loaded from fonts.googleapis.com; their privacy policy applies.

Cookies and analytics

No marketing or analytics tracking scripts are included. No cookies are set by this site.

Changes

This notice must be updated before enabling email marketing, analytics, payments, merchandise checkout or additional processors.
